# Linear-congruence-system
Solver for congruence equations and systems. Done for learning discrete mathematics.
Inputs a system of the form:
$$
\begin{cases}
& a_1x \equiv b_1 (mod n_1) \\
& a_2x \equiv b_2 (mod n_2) \\
& (\vdots) \\
& a_nx \equiv b_n (mod n_n) \\
\end{cases}
$$
It reduces the equations and solves them with the Chinese Remainder Theorem (if a solution exists).
